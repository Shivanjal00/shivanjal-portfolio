A bit about how I work

I enjoy the whole path an app takes, not just one part of it.

I started with Android and Java, moved to Kotlin, and then picked up React Native so one codebase could ship to both stores. That mix lets me choose the right tool for the job instead of forcing one.

Most of my time goes into the parts users never see but always feel: smooth lists, background audio that keeps playing, live scores that update on time, and updates that don't break anything.
